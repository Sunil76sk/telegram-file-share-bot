# Telegram File Share Bot — Compliance & Content Moderation Guidelines

To ensure the longevity of the bot and protect it from bans, copyright strikes, and hosting suspensions, administrators must adhere to these compliance guidelines.

---

## 🚫 Prohibited Activities & Content
The distribution or generation of links for the following items is **strictly prohibited**:

* ❌ **Copyright Infringement & Piracy**: Distributing paid movies, TV shows, music, paid software cracks, keygens, or premium games.
* ❌ **Pirated Content Distribution**: Reposting copyrighted database files or course materials without explicit licensing.
* ❌ **Illegal Leaks**: Sharing private databases, leaked confidential documentation, or non-public personal information.
* ❌ **Malware Distribution**: Hosting ransomware, spyware, adware, keyloggers, or phishing scripts.
* ❌ **Scam APK Sharing**: Modded Android application packages (APKs) that contain telemetry bypasses, adware, or credential stealers.

---

## ✅ Approved Content & Use Cases
The bot is optimized for sharing safe, legal, and original creator content, including:

* 🟢 **Educational Resources**: E-books, study guides, worksheets, open-source textbooks, and learning resources.
* 🟢 **AI Tools & Assets**: AI prompts, custom model weights, trained configurations, datasets, and AI-generated media packs.
* 🟢 **Templates & Presets**: Website templates (HTML/CSS), design layouts, Figma assets, spreadsheet spreadsheets, and resume templates.
* 🟢 **Editing Assets**: Copyright-free stock footages, overlay packs, lightroom presets, LUTs, sound effects (SFX), and font packs.
* 🟢 **Tutorials & Courses**: Original video courses, step-by-step guides, walkthroughs, and code repositories.
* 🟢 **Community Content**: Community newsletters, event materials, wallpapers, and memes.
* 🟢 **Original Creator Content**: Portfolios, personal art, original music, and indie-game demos.

---

## 🛠 Best Practices for Admins & Moderation
1. **Immediate Revocation (`/edit_link`)**:
   - If a link is flagged for hosting prohibited material, use `/edit_link <code> delete` to instantly destroy the shared entry and prevent further downloads.
2. **Access Log Checks (`/accesslogs`)**:
   - Keep track of unusually high traffic links using `/accesslogs <token>` to identify potentially viral pirated content early.
3. **Database Maintenance**:
   - Periodically clean up expired and inactive shared items to keep database storage footprint optimized and safe.
